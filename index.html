    How to play

    The aim

    Race: gather more acorns than the blue jays, groundhogs and grey squirrels before the park is picked clean. Your best haul is kept.

    Explore: no clock and no end. Whenever an acorn is taken, another grows somewhere out of sight, so wander the park and gather as many as you like. Leave from the pause menu.

    Moving

    On a phone or tablet, put a finger down anywhere and drag: a stick appears under it. On a computer, use the arrow keys or WASD, or drag with the mouse.

    The pause button (or Esc or P) stops the game and opens a menu to resume, restart, read this, or go back to the start. The speaker turns sound on and off.

    The thieves

    Blue jay: circles over an acorn with a chirp, then swoops down for it. Stand by the acorn and it gives up.

    Groundhog: heaves up a mound of dirt with a rumble, peeks out, then climbs out for the acorn. Get close and it ducks back down.

    Grey squirrel: can't be scared off, but is slower than you. Get there first.

    Finding acorns

    Blinking arrows at the edge of the screen point to the nearest acorns, and the map in the corner shows where you are.

    The stream can only be crossed on the log bridges.

    A hedged field in the south-west corner has two ways in, a gate and a gap. Its long grass slows you down, and acorns in it show only their tops.

    A golden acorn is hidden somewhere in the park. It doesn't count in the race, but see if you can find it.

    Playing with friends

    Play with up to three friends, each on their own phone or computer. Everyone presses Play with friends and types a name. One presses Host a game and gets a four-letter code; the others press Join a game and type it in, or open the link the host shares. The host picks Race or Explore.

    The internet is needed to connect; after that the phones talk directly. Each squirrel has its own colour, and you see the others on screen and on the map. There are no thieves when you play with friends. Pausing on any phone pauses them all, and so does switching away from the game.

    A friend can join part-way through a round, starting at the crossing. If someone leaves, the rest play on.
